# Acme Fabrication Co. — ESG Questionnaire Response (FY2025)

*Fictional sample document for demonstration purposes.*

## Section A — Governance

A1. Does the board have oversight of climate-related issues?
Yes. The Audit & Risk Committee charter was amended in March 2025 to include
"climate-related risks to operations and supply" as a standing agenda item,
reviewed twice per year. The most recent review occurred in May 2025.

A2. Is there management-level ownership?
The VP of Operations holds executive responsibility for the climate program.
There is currently no dedicated sustainability role; the company states it
intends to hire a Sustainability Manager in FY2026.

A3. Are climate considerations linked to remuneration?
No. No executive or management incentives are currently tied to climate or
emissions performance.

## Section B — Environmental Management

B1. Is there a certified environmental management system?
The Willow Bend facility holds ISO 14001 certification (certificate issued
October 2023, next surveillance audit due October 2026). Other facilities are
not certified.

B2. Does the company have an emissions reduction target?
The company has publicly stated an ambition to "reduce operational emissions
intensity by 20% by 2030" on its website. No baseline year, boundary, or
methodology for this ambition has been documented, and it has not been
approved by the board.

B3. Energy management practices?
LED retrofits were completed at Alder Point and Cedar Landing in FY2024.
Sub-metering exists only at Sycamore Park. There is no facility-level energy
management standard.

## Section C — Physical Risk & Continuity

C1. Has the company assessed physical climate risk to its facilities?
An insurance broker review in 2021 flagged riverine flood exposure at two
River Valley facilities. No forward-looking (scenario-based) assessment has
been performed to date.

C2. Business continuity provisions?
Sycamore Park (data center) maintains N+1 generator redundancy with 72 hours
of on-site fuel. Manufacturing facilities have documented continuity plans
last exercised in 2022.

## Section D — Supply Chain

D1. Supplier code of conduct?
A supplier code including environmental clauses was issued in January 2025;
as of June 2025, 61% of tier-1 suppliers by spend have signed. No audit
program exists yet.
