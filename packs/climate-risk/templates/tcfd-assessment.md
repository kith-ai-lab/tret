# TCFD-Aligned Climate Risk Assessment — {{company}}

Deliverable slug: `tcfd-assessment`. Sections are drafted individually via the
`tcfd_section_draft` task and assembled on export.

## governance

Board and management oversight of climate-related risks and opportunities:
who owns the topic, how often it reaches the board, what expertise exists,
and how climate is embedded in incentives and policies. Ground claims in
extracted governance evidence; distinguish documented practice from
client-asserted intent.

## strategy

Material climate-related risks and opportunities over the short, medium, and
long term: physical exposure of key sites (draw on recorded divergence
verdicts and hazard data), transition exposures (policy, market, technology),
and the resilience of the strategy under different climate futures. State the
scenarios considered and their horizons.

## risk_management

How climate risks are identified, assessed, and managed, and how that process
integrates with enterprise risk management. Describe the assessment method
honestly, including where forward-looking signals and vendor scores diverged
and how those gaps are being handled.

## metrics_targets

The metrics and targets used to assess and manage climate risks: emissions
baseline (scopes and boundaries), intensity measures, reduction targets and
their status, and any site-level risk metrics. Every figure must trace to
extracted evidence or retrieved data; declare gaps rather than estimating.
