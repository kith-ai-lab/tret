# GHG Baseline Narrative — {{company}}

Deliverable slug: `ghg-baseline-narrative`.

## boundary_and_method

Organizational and operational boundaries, the consolidation approach, and
the calculation method, stated per the GHG Protocol vocabulary. Name the
activity-data sources and emission-factor sets used, and their vintages.

## scope_1

Direct emissions by source category (stationary combustion, mobile
combustion, fugitives), with the activity data behind each figure. Declare
excluded sources and why.

## scope_2

Purchased energy emissions, location-based and (where contracts exist)
market-based, with the grid factors used and their vintage.

## data_quality_and_gaps

An honest account of data quality: which figures rest on metered data, which
on invoices or estimates, what was missing, and what data requests are open.
This section is mandatory — a baseline without a stated quality tier is not a
deliverable.
